THE MATH

Same purchase. Two rates.

How to check a card conversion on your statement

Reference amount = purchase × reference rate. Effective rate = actual charge ÷ purchase. Percentage difference = (actual charge − reference amount) ÷ reference amount × 100.

THE DATE

Keep both dates.

We show the returned reference date, even when it differs from your purchase date. Weekend and holiday rates can use earlier observations. Blended rates can change as sources are revised; save the JSON to retain the rate used.

THE PRIVACY

Your amounts stay local.

Live lookups send the currency pair, date and selected provider to Frankfurter. The Bank of Canada converter requests the date range and public series. Your charge, purchase amount and note are not sent. Manual comparisons need no rate request. Nothing is saved in browser storage.

THE SOURCES

Follow the original.

Frankfurter API · Rate methodology · Provider terms. Blended references are not official fixings. Provider coverage varies by pair and year. Displayed money is rounded for its currency; exports retain calculation precision.
